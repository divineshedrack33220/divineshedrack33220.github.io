github.com/…/globmint ↗ Go · Solidity · Flutter

GlobMint

Creator — self-custodial stablecoin savings & payments

Move USDC without banks or fiat rails. Every account gets its own on-chain vault — deterministic EIP-1167 clones deployed by a CREATE2 factory, so funds are receivable at a unique address with no wallet-connect required. Backend is an indexer + ledger + withdrawal-intent relay; contracts have no owner or admin.

  • Non-custodial by construction — no admin key can freeze funds
  • Go API relays signed withdrawals; indexer credits the ledger from chain events
  • Live on Sepolia testnet, staged for Ethereum mainnet
