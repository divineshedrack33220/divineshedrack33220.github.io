CASE STUDY · REAL-TIME SOCIAL

Zukaping

Real-time social discovery with location-based matchmaking. Live presence, instant chat, and a WebSocket hub that keeps everyone in sync — Flutter on the client, Go on the wire.

The problem

Social and dating apps live or die on latency. Polling kills presence; generic chat backends can't handle matchmaking state; cross-platform clients usually mean two codebases.

The approach

Zukaping runs a Go/Gin backend with a WebSocket hub for presence and messaging, a location-based matching pipeline that scores nearby users in real time, and a single Flutter codebase for iOS, Android and web. When a match is found, a direct room opens over the same socket layer — no polling, no third-party chat SDK.

  • WebSocket hub: presence, typing, chat delivery at low latency
  • Location-based matchmaking pipeline (radius scoring)
  • Direct encrypted rooms opened on match
  • Flutter + Riverpod client — one codebase, three platforms
  • MongoDB for flexible social graph and message history

Stack

Flutter · Dart · Riverpod · Go · Gin · WebSockets · MongoDB.

Outcome

A real-time matching system where presence and chat feel instant.
