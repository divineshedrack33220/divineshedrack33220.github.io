CASE STUDY · LIVE ON SEPOLIA

GlobMint

Self-custodial stablecoin savings and payments on Ethereum — no banks, no fiat rails. Every account gets its own on-chain USDC vault. No owner. No admin. No one who can freeze your money.

The problem

Crypto products usually ask users to trust a company with their keys — or force wallet-connect flows that confuse newcomers. Banks and fiat rails add KYC, delays and counterparty risk.

The approach

GlobMint is non-custodial by construction. A CREATE2 factory deploys an EIP-1167 minimal proxy vault per account, so every user has a deterministic, unique on-chain address that can receive USDC from anyone — no wallet connection needed to deposit. The vault contracts have no owner and no admin functions: funds cannot be seized or frozen by anyone, including the developer.

  • Per-user vault clones (EIP-1167) via CREATE2 factory — deterministic addresses
  • Ownerless Solidity contracts — no admin key exists
  • Go API: indexer watches chain events, credits an internal ledger for fast balances
  • Withdrawal intents relayed and signed by the backend signer
  • Flutter client (Riverpod) for web and mobile

Stack

Go · Solidity (EVM, CREATE2, EIP-1167) · Flutter + Riverpod · PostgreSQL 16 · Ethereum Sepolia (mainnet staged).

Outcome

Live on Sepolia testnet. Users hold and move USDC without banks, without wallet-connect gymnastics, and without trusting anyone with custody.
